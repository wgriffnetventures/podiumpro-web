Privacy policy

Your work. Your device.

PodiumPro saves rehearsal information locally. Sharing is your choice.

Last updated: October 8, 2026 · Developer: {{OWNER}}

What this policy covers

This policy covers the PodiumPro app, this website, and support correspondence with its developer. It describes the current app’s behavior. No PodiumPro account is required.

Information stored by the app

The app stores plan names and dates, segment titles, durations, start times, colors, focus notes, rehearsal timing and event records, reflections, and settings in local app storage.

If you choose a forScore CSV or a PodiumPro document, the app reads that file and saves the accepted metadata or document content locally. Repertoire metadata can include titles, composers, and source filenames. The CSV import does not import score PDFs or annotations.

This information supports planning, timing, reviewing, and reusing rehearsals. The developer does not receive it automatically. The app does not include advertising, behavioral analytics, tracking, or automatic uploads to a developer-operated server.

Files, sharing, and device backups

The app uses the operating system’s file picker and share sheet when you request an import, export, or share. Selected files may come from a cloud file provider. Shared plans and reports can contain titles, notes, timing records, and reflections. Review the contents before sending them.

Information you send or save through another app or provider is handled by that recipient or provider under its own terms and privacy policy. PodiumPro does not automatically send your rehearsal information to those services.

Your operating system may include local app information in device backups according to your settings. PodiumPro does not provide automatic cross-device synchronization.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you remove it or remove the app’s storage. You can delete saved plans in Plans and completed records in Past rehearsals. Refreshing repertoire replaces the saved repertoire list but does not remove titles already added to plans.

Local migration backups and temporary export/report files can remain in app storage. Removing the app from iPhone or iPad removes its local app storage. Exported files, shared copies, and operating-system or cloud backups are separate; manage or delete them through the relevant device settings, file provider, or recipient.

The developer cannot remotely access or delete data that stays only on your device. For information you voluntarily send to support, you can request deletion using the contact below.

Support correspondence

If you contact support, the developer receives your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. This information is used to respond, investigate the issue, and improve the app. Send only information needed to explain your request.

The email provider processes correspondence to deliver and store it. Support information is retained only as needed to resolve requests and maintain relevant support records, or meet applicable obligations. You can request deletion of your correspondence and attachments.

Visits to this website

The site does not include advertising pixels, third-party analytics scripts, or embedded social-media widgets. Fonts and page assets are served with the site.

The website is intended to be hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not visitors are signed in. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for its practices. This is separate from rehearsal information stored by the app.

Choices and updates

You choose which files to import and whether to export, share, or contact support. You can use the app without sharing your plans with the developer. Do not put sensitive information into notes or reflections unless you are comfortable storing it on your device and including it in any reports you share.

If these practices change, this policy will be updated with a new date. Material app changes will be reflected in the policy and relevant App Store disclosures.
