PodiumPro support

Back to the music.

Quick answers for planning, conducting, and reviewing a rehearsal.

Build and reuse a plan

Give the plan a name, optionally set its date and start time, then add segments. Edit names and minutes directly in the schedule. Use the drag handles to reorder. Open Plans to switch plans or duplicate a plan for another rehearsal.

Use a segment’s ••• menu to change its type or color. On portrait phones, empty Focus/Notes fields are collapsed; choose Show Focus or Show Notes in that menu to add them. Hiding a field preserves its text.

Import repertoire from forScore
  1. In forScore, open Backups and use the scrolled-page icon to export metadata as a CSV.
  2. In PodiumPro, choose Repertoire, then Choose forScore CSV.
  3. Review the preview and accept the import. Search by title or composer, select titles, and add them to the plan.

Use UTF-8 encoding. A UTF-16 export needs to be re-exported with forScore’s CSV encoding setting set to UTF-8. Refresh CSV replaces the saved repertoire list after confirmation; it preserves existing plan segments.

This imports metadata only. Score files and annotations remain in forScore. PodiumPro is independent of forScore.

Move plans between devices

Export a plan as a .podiumpro file. Transfer it using Files, AirDrop, or another method you choose. On the receiving device, import the file and review the preview. Add plan creates a separate library entry, preserving existing plans.

PodiumPro does not automatically synchronize devices or run a paired timer across them.

Change a rehearsal while it is running

Pause or resume the countdown, advance with Next, or jump to another agenda item. When a segment begins, the agenda scrolls to it; you can browse manually between advances. In phone landscape, use Up next to jump or open Agenda for the full list.

Use Add time on compact screens, or the extension buttons on larger layouts. The pencil lets you change a current segment’s duration while preserving elapsed time. Reducing the duration below elapsed time produces overtime.

Settings is available during a rehearsal. Show wall clock and Keep screen awake are saved preferences. Opening Settings does not pause the timer.

Understand warnings and overtime

The countdown displays a two-finger cue at two minutes remaining and a one-finger cue at one minute. Light mode uses a yellow warning panel, stronger in the final minute. After the budget is exhausted, the display switches to an overtime cue. These are visual cues; do not rely on an audible alarm.

Saving, interruptions, and recovery

PodiumPro saves locally as you work. After a cold restart, a recovered rehearsal resumes in a paused state so you can review it before continuing. If a save error appears, use Retry and preserve important work with an export when possible.

Export important plans and rehearsal records before removing the app or changing devices. Device backups may retain data according to your operating-system settings.

Share a report or delete saved work

End the rehearsal to review timing and add a reflection. Share a text or PDF report through the system share sheet. Past rehearsals also offers sharing, document export, and deletion.

Delete saved plans from Plans and saved rehearsal records from Past rehearsals. Exports and shared copies must be removed from wherever you saved or sent them. Removing the app from iPhone or iPad removes its local app storage; backups may keep separate copies.
